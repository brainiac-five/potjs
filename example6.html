POT JS Example 6: Network Parboiled

	Check source and console.


	This page connects to a local Swarm network.

	It works like example 5 but can be tried by simply
	running `make example6`, which takes care of the local
	network and the batch id.

	What the make rules do is essentially:

	fdp-play start --detach	
	swarm-cli stamp buy --yes --verbose --depth 20 --amount 1b > .batch_creation
	grep "Stamp ID:" .batch_creation | cut -c11-74 > .batch_id
	npx http-server -c1 . &
	open "http://127.0.0.1:8080/example6.html?batch=$(cat .batch_id)"

	Use `make stop` and `make locnet_stop` to stop servers.
